About gurukultuition (Gurukul Home Tuitions)

gurukultuition (gurukultuition.vercel.app) is Kathmandu Valley's premier educational network specializing in 1-on-1 private home tutors, Gurukul tuition, and small-group KTM tuition batches. Whether your child is preparing for SEE board exams, +2 Science/Management entrance, or needs daily conceptual guidance from Grade 1 through Bachelor level, our verified tutors deliver results right at your doorstep across Kathmandu, Lalitpur, and Bhaktapur.

Teacher's policy
  • Not to be absent in classes without giving prior information to parents. If done, parents may deduct salary or ask for a substitute day for taking class.
  • Not to ask for more salary than what has been provided in the vacancy posted by us. If found to be done, they will be immediately terminated by tuition and no refund will be granted.
  • Has to pay 35% of the salary as commission to us prior to the commencement of demo classes, which will only be refunded in the following cases:
    • If parents are not satisfied by the teacher after the demo class and hence they reject the teacher. (In this case NPR 200 will be deducted while refunding.)
    • If the tuition gets cancelled by us due to some reason, full amount will be refunded.
    • If the demo class could not take place due to the reason of parents or us, then the whole amount is to be refunded.
    • In case the tuition is cancelled by the teacher's side, then no refund will be provided.
  • Has to provide demo classes of 2 days to the parents; if parents request 1 extra day of demo class then it should be provided.
  • Not to request parents to reject them on purpose or purposely do something to get rejected — if found to be done, they might face legal action for defaming our reputation.
  • Refund process might take 2–3 days in order to analyze all the cases of refund; please wait patiently and talk politely.
